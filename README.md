Shukhee Customer Growth & Marketing Analytics
An independent internship assessment exploring customer acquisition, conversion, retention, and marketing performance for Shukhee, a digital health platform in Bangladesh.
Note: All customer records, marketing metrics, spending, and revenue figures are illustrative dummy data, not actual Shukhee business data.

Project Deliverables
- SQL: PostgreSQL queries for inactive customers, average order value ranking, customer segmentation, and monthly order growth.
- Excel: Three-month Facebook Ads and Google Ads dataset, KPI calculations, summaries, charts, performance gaps, and budget analysis.
- Presentation: Five-slide summary of findings and recommendations.
Key Findings
Based on the illustrative August–October 2026 dataset:
- Facebook generated more downloads (58,500), while Google generated more first orders (4,284) and revenue (৳3,863,600).
- Google had higher registration-to-first-order conversion (30% vs. 18.7%) and repeat-order rate (40% vs. 30%).
- Google recorded higher ROAS (4.69x vs. 3.11x).
Recommendations
1. Improve the registration-to-first-order journey, especially for Facebook-acquired users.
2. Reduce booking friction and address cancellations, delays, and customer complaints.
3. Use relevant, consent-based reminders to encourage repeat transactions.
4. Test budget changes gradually and monitor conversion, repeat rate, ROAS, and incremental profit.

Limitations
The data is synthetic and intended only to demonstrate an analysis approach. Customer summary records do not fully reconcile with the sample order records. Channel comparisons are descriptive, not proof of causation. ROI and ROAS do not account for service delivery costs or operating expenses.

Project status: Completed as an independent assessment. No live Shukhee systems, customer records, or marketing campaigns were accessed.
